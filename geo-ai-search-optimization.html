What is GEO / AEO for limo companies?

GEO and AEO are ways of preparing a limo, chauffeur, black car or airport transportation business to be clearly understood and usefully represented in AI-powered search and answer experiences. In practice, that means strong SEO foundations, unambiguous entity and service information, useful answer-style content, technical accessibility, evidence, structured relationships and measurement of the visibility that current webmaster tools actually expose.
