Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains the types of information that may be collected when you use The Limo Marketing Agency website, submit a consultation request or otherwise interact with us online, and how that information may be used and protected.

Privacy in Plain Language

We may collect information you choose to send us, such as consultation details, together with limited technical information generated when you use the website. We use that information to respond to requests, operate and improve the website, understand marketing performance and support legitimate business operations.

1. Scope

This Privacy Policy applies to information collected through The Limo Marketing Agency website and related online forms or digital interactions controlled by us. It does not automatically apply to websites, platforms or services operated by third parties.

2. Information We Collect

Information you provide

When you submit a consultation request, contact us or otherwise provide information through the website, we may receive information such as:

  • Your name and company name.
  • Email address and phone number.
  • Website address, business type and primary market.
  • Services you are interested in.
  • Information you choose to include about your business, marketing, website, booking process or current challenges.

Information generated through website use

Depending on the analytics, advertising and technical tools connected to the website, limited technical or usage information may be generated, such as browser type, device information, approximate location derived from network information, pages viewed, referral source, interaction events and similar website-performance data.

3. How We Use Information

Information may be used for legitimate business purposes including:

  • Reviewing and responding to consultation or contact requests.
  • Understanding a prospective client's business needs.
  • Providing requested information about our services.
  • Operating, maintaining and improving the website.
  • Measuring website and marketing performance.
  • Detecting abuse, fraud, technical issues or security risks.
  • Complying with legal obligations where applicable.

4. Cookies & Analytics

The website may use cookies or similar technologies to support functionality, understand website usage and measure marketing performance. Analytics or advertising tools connected at launch may create or read identifiers in accordance with their own settings and policies.

Where consent is required by applicable law, non-essential cookies or similar technologies should be activated only after the appropriate consent choice has been provided.

The final production cookie banner and consent configuration should match the analytics and advertising tools actually connected to the live website.

5. Service Providers

We may use third-party service providers to help operate the website or business, such as hosting, analytics, communications, scheduling, customer relationship management, advertising or security services. These providers may process information only to the extent necessary to provide their services or as otherwise permitted under their own agreements and applicable law.

We do not state that every category listed above is currently connected. The specific providers used may change as the website and business systems evolve.

6. Advertising & Marketing Measurement

If advertising platforms are connected to the website, interaction or conversion events may be used to understand whether campaigns lead to meaningful actions such as consultation requests, calls or other customer activity. Advertising and measurement settings should be configured according to applicable privacy requirements and platform policies.

7. Data Retention

We may retain information for as long as reasonably necessary to respond to requests, maintain business records, operate our services, resolve disputes, enforce agreements or meet legal obligations. Retention periods can vary depending on the type of information and why it was collected.

8. Security

We use reasonable administrative, technical and organizational measures intended to protect information against unauthorized access, loss, misuse or alteration. No internet transmission or storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure, so absolute security cannot be promised.

9. Your Choices & Privacy Rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights concerning personal information, such as requesting access, correction, deletion, restriction or other controls provided by applicable law. You may also be able to manage certain browser cookies or advertising preferences through browser and platform settings.

To make a privacy-related request, use the contact method provided in the Contact section below. We may need to verify the request before taking action.

10. International Visitors

The website may be accessed from different countries. If information is processed using service providers located in another country, privacy protections and legal requirements may differ from those in your location. Where required, appropriate safeguards should be used for international data handling.

11. Children's Privacy

This website is intended for business and professional audiences and is not designed for children. We do not intentionally use the website to solicit personal information from children.

12. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy as the website, services, technology or legal requirements change. When material updates are made, the date shown at the top of this page should be revised.

13. Privacy Questions or Requests

If you have a privacy question or want to make a request concerning information submitted through this website, contact The Limo Marketing Agency through the website contact page.
