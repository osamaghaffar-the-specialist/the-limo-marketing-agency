## Operating Rules

1. Prefer relationship-based or editorially earned mentions over paid link placement.
2. Never request exact-match keyword anchors, sitewide footer links, fake reviews, fake partnerships or reciprocal-link schemes.
3. Keep the destination URL aligned with the keyword-owner map.
4. Do not publicly identify a client relationship without permission. Existing-client opportunities are anonymized in this public repository.
5. A paid membership/directory is justified only by real industry visibility, networking or referral value — not by “DA/DR” or a promised backlink.
6. Vendor/integration outreach stays on HOLD until the integration or implementation is real and can be demonstrated truthfully.
7. Record outreach date, reply, live referring URL and destination URL after each real action.

## Outreach Sequence

### 1. Existing client permission request — relationship-first

**Subject:** Can we document this implementation as a short case study?

Hi [Name],

We have been documenting the website / booking work completed for your business. I would like to turn the implementation into a short, factual case study showing the original problem, what was built, and the parts you are comfortable verifying publicly.

Nothing would be published without your approval, and we would not share private business data or performance figures unless you explicitly approve them.

If you are open to it, I can send you the draft for approval first.

### 2. Editorial pitch — Chauffeur Driven / PHTM

**Subject:** Contributor idea: SEO architecture that avoids thin airport and city pages

Hi [Editor],

I work specifically on growth and booking infrastructure for limo, chauffeur and private-hire operators. I would like to contribute a practical, non-promotional piece on how operators can structure airport, route, service and city pages around real search demand without creating thin doorway-style pages.

The article would include a usable audit framework, examples of page ownership and internal-link structure, and clear limitations — no ranking promises or sales pitch.

If this fits your editorial calendar, I can send a concise outline first.

### 3. Industry membership / directory qualification — NLA / PHTM

**Subject:** Supplier eligibility and directory/profile information

Hi,

The Limo Marketing Agency provides marketing, conversion-focused websites and booking/workflow infrastructure for chauffeured-transport operators. Before applying or purchasing a supplier listing, I would like to confirm the current eligibility requirements, profile/directory visibility, and any education or contributor opportunities available to suppliers.

Could you share the current supplier terms and the best route to apply?

### 4. Vendor case-study / integration pitch — only after a real implementation

**Subject:** Operator booking-to-dispatch integration case study

Hi [Vendor],

We have implemented a customer-facing acquisition/booking workflow for an operator that hands bookings into [Vendor] through an approved integration path. We can document the architecture, testing process and operator use case without exposing private customer data.

Would your partnerships or content team be interested in reviewing a joint implementation case study?

**Do not send this template until the integration is real, approved and testable.**
