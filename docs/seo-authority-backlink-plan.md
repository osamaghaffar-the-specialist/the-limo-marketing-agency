# TLMA Authority & Backlink Acquisition Plan — US + UK

**Date:** 2026-10-10  
**Purpose:** Build topical authority for The Limo Marketing Agency without spammy link building, fabricated partnerships, or irrelevant directory blasts.

## Evidence-based diagnosis

The indexed SEO service page is already semantically aligned with its target family (`SEO for limo companies`, `limo SEO`, `limo service SEO`, `limo company SEO`). Current GSC visibility is mainly US-led, but average positions remain deep. This means the next growth lever should not be another title/H1 rewrite; it should be discovery, external authority, verified proof, and relevant third-party mentions.

## Principles

1. Prefer links from limo, chauffeur, private-hire, taxi, transportation-software and adjacent business ecosystems.
2. One strong industry mention is more valuable than dozens of unrelated low-quality directories.
3. Never buy bulk backlink packages, PBN links, automated guest posts or fake review placements.
4. Do not claim membership, partnership, certification or integration until it is real and verifiable.
5. Case-study links must describe work that actually happened and use verified outcomes only.
6. Supplier/member-directory links are useful when the organization is relevant and the listing is legitimate, but they are not a substitute for editorial links.
7. Create linkable assets around operator decisions: dispatch pricing, booking-system ownership, integration architecture, Google Ads benchmarks/methodology, and technical SEO audits.

## Tier 1 — Highest-value authority opportunities

### A. Client implementation / case-study links
**Priority:** P1  
**Cost:** Usually relationship-based  
**Target:** Existing or future limo/chauffeur/black-car clients.

Ask for a contextual attribution on a real implementation, for example:
- Website / booking technology partner
- Marketing / SEO partner
- Case study collaborator
- Booking integration implementation

Best destination URL depends on the work performed:
- SEO implementation → `/seo-for-limo-companies.html`
- Google Ads → `/google-ads-for-limo-companies.html`
- Website → `/limo-website-design.html`
- Booking system → `/booking-systems.html`
- Broad agency attribution → `/`

**Guardrail:** Never create site-wide footer keyword links across client sites. Prefer one natural About/Partners/Case Study attribution.

### B. Partner / vendor case studies
**Priority:** P1  
**Target ecosystem:** booking, dispatch, CRM, payments, communications, maps and automation vendors used in real projects.

Potential future outreach when a verified implementation exists:
- TaxiCaller
- iCabbi
- Autocab
- Cordic
- payment / CRM / automation vendors actually used by TLMA

Pitch angle: an implementation story showing how acquisition traffic reaches a booking flow and hands off into the operator's existing stack.

**Guardrail:** Do not publish an “integration” page that implies official partnership or technical support unless it is actually implemented and verified.

### C. Original operator research assets
**Priority:** P1  
**Goal:** Earn citations rather than ask for generic links.

Best planned assets:
1. UK Taxi Dispatch System Pricing — vendor-sourced, dated cost table.
2. Best Taxi Dispatch Software UK — transparent methodology and fleet-size segmentation.
3. SaaS Booking Software vs Custom Booking System — ownership, fees, integrations, UX and data-control matrix.
4. Limo Google Ads Benchmark Methodology — only from verified account data or clearly labelled public-source analysis.
5. Limo SEO Technical Audit Checklist — crawl/index/local/booking conversion framework.

Each asset should have:
- retrieval/update date
- source links
- methodology
- explicit limitations
- no invented scores or results

## Tier 2 — Industry organizations and directories

### National Limousine Association (NLA)
**Market:** US / global chauffeured transportation  
**Type:** Industry association with operator and supplier/manufacturer membership; member resources include a vendor member directory.

Current public membership information shows a Supplier category and an international supplier rate. Treat membership as a business-development/authority decision, not as a backlink purchase.

**Action:** Evaluate supplier membership ROI only after the main domain is live and the company profile, proof and contact infrastructure are ready.

### Chauffeur Driven
**Market:** US / global chauffeured transportation  
**Type:** Industry publication and operator ecosystem.

The site runs Affiliate Central and describes itself as a leading chauffeured-ground-transportation industry resource. Operator directory listings are not the same as an agency/supplier listing, so do not force-fit TLMA into an operator directory.

**Action:** Prioritize editorial contribution, event/vendor opportunities, expert commentary or genuinely relevant supplier exposure over an irrelevant operator listing.

### PHTM Supplier Directory
**Market:** UK taxi / private hire  
**Type:** Supplier directory with categories including consultancy, social media, data dispatch, taxi booking apps, fleet-management platforms and other operator services.

**Action:** Verify current supplier-listing eligibility, pricing and whether marketing/consultancy is an appropriate category before applying.

## First 30-day authority sprint

### Week 1 — Foundation
- Finalize real domain before paid association/listing spend where possible.
- Create one consistent company description and service taxonomy.
- Prepare a compact media/vendor profile: logo, short bio, services, target industries, contact, website.
- Inventory every real client/vendor relationship that could support a case study or attribution.

### Week 2 — Owned proof + outreach assets
- Choose one verified implementation case study.
- Produce one linkable technical resource (preferably a checklist/methodology asset that does not require invented data).
- Prepare 3 outreach emails: client attribution, vendor case study, expert contribution.

### Week 3 — Industry outreach
- Contact relevant client/partner relationships first.
- Verify PHTM supplier listing requirements.
- Evaluate NLA supplier membership only on broader ROI (networking + visibility + vendor ecosystem), not “link value” alone.
- Identify Chauffeur Driven editorial/vendor contact path for an educational contribution.

### Week 4 — Measure
Track:
- new referring domains
- exact referring page
- destination URL
- anchor text
- topical relevance
- whether the link is crawlable
- new GSC impressions/queries to the linked page over time

## 90-day target quality mix

Do not set a fake “100 backlinks” target. A healthier goal is a small number of real, relevant referring domains across these buckets:
- real clients / implementation partners
- transportation industry organizations/publications
- software/vendor ecosystem mentions
- original-research citations
- a limited set of reputable business/agency profiles

Quality is judged by relevance, editorial context, legitimacy and referral potential—not DA/DR alone.

## Release gate for any backlink opportunity

Before pursuing or paying for a placement, confirm:
- Is the site relevant to transportation, software, marketing, or the target buyer?
- Is the placement legitimate and visible to real users?
- Is the company/entity description truthful?
- Is the destination URL the correct keyword owner?
- Would we still want the mention if Google ignored the link?

If the last answer is “no,” it is probably not a strong authority opportunity.
