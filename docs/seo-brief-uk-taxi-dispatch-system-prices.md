## 1. Why This Page Is First

This is the first evidence-backed new-page candidate from the external keyword-gap war map because the project’s verified Semrush snapshot recorded:

- `taxi dispatch system prices` — volume ~110, KD 9;
- `taxi dispatch system prices uk` — volume ~90, KD 12, CPC ~£3.95;

The page must **not** present TLMA as a taxi dispatch software vendor. Its role is an independent, source-led buyer guide that helps UK taxi/private-hire operators understand total dispatch-system cost and then offers a **Free Dispatch Cost Audit**.

## 3. Search Intent

The searcher is usually comparing systems before contacting vendors or switching platforms. They want to know:

1. what the software costs;
2. whether pricing is per driver, per vehicle, per week/month, or quote-only;
3. what is included;
4. which extra fees can materially change total cost;
5. what a realistic first-year cost looks like for their fleet size;
6. whether a cheaper published plan is truly cheaper after apps, SMS, phone, API, onboarding and migration costs;
7. whether they need full dispatch software or only a booking/conversion layer.

This is commercial investigation, not a generic educational article.

## 5. Direct-Answer Opening Block

The first 120–180 words should answer the query immediately:

- there is no single UK market price;
- vendors use different pricing units (driver, vehicle, flat monthly, weekly, package or quote-only);
- published entry prices currently range from very low-cost per-driver models to packaged fleet plans, while major enterprise platforms often require a tailored quote;
- the headline subscription is not enough: apps, SMS/telephony, white-labelling, payment fees, API/integration, migration, onboarding and contract terms can change total cost materially;
- this guide separates **vendor-published prices** from **quote-only or third-party estimates**.

Do not use an unsupported “average UK cost” in the opening.

## 6. Evidence Standard

Every pricing claim must be classified into one of these buckets:

### A. Vendor-published price
Use exact figures only when the vendor or authorised reseller publishes them on a live page.

### B. Vendor pricing model, exact amount not public
Example: per-driver / per-vehicle / tailored quote is confirmed, but no exact current amount is displayed.

### C. Third-party indicative figure
May be discussed only when useful, clearly labelled **indicative / third-party reported**, dated, and never presented as the vendor’s official price.

### D. Quote-only / unknown
Say `Contact vendor for current quote`. Never fill the gap with an estimate.

All source rows must have a `Last checked` date.

## 8. Required Comparison Table on the Future Page

The live page should use a transparent table with these columns:

`Vendor | Pricing unit | Published starting price | Drivers/vehicles included | Setup fee | Contract/minimum term | Fare commission | App/white-label extras | SMS/phone/API extras | Source type | Last checked`

Rules:

- never put a third-party estimate in the same visual style as a vendor-published figure without a label;
- use `Quote required` where appropriate;
- keep VAT treatment visible where the source specifies it;
- do not normalize weekly to monthly using an arbitrary 4-week multiplier without explaining the calculation basis;
- do not compare a bare dispatch-only plan against a full communications/app package as if they were equivalent.
