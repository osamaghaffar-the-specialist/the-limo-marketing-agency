## Measurement Rules

For every future response or placement, record only what can be verified:

- response date and exact outcome;
- whether the editor requested an outline, draft, interview or declined;
- whether any commercial fee is involved;
- exact live referring URL if something is published;
- exact TLMA destination URL;
- natural anchor/brand wording used by the publisher;
- whether the link is crawlable after publication;
- referral traffic/leads if measurable;
- GSC changes as observation only, without claiming that one link caused ranking movement.

## Guardrails

- A sent pitch is not a backlink.
- A directory inquiry is not membership or a partnership.
- Do not request exact-match anchor text.
- Do not pay for bulk/PBN/automated guest-post links.
- Do not publish client identities, results or integrations without permission and evidence.
- Keep TaxiCaller/vendor-integration outreach on hold until a real approved implementation exists.
