## Free authority channels verified on 2026-10-12

### Priority A — create first

1. **LinkedIn Company Page** — free company page; use exact brand name, official website, logo, tagline and service description.
2. **LinkedIn Service Page** — free service landing page; useful because LinkedIn states Service Pages can be discoverable through search engines and members can contact providers.
3. **Crunchbase company profile** — registered users can create/edit a company profile at no cost; use only factual company details.
4. **Clutch Basic profile** — free company profile; complete service focus, overview and website fields. Do not buy verification at this stage.
5. **GoodFirms listing** — free company listing; keep descriptions and service categories consistent with the website.

### Priority B — after Priority A is consistent

6. **Agency Spotter Free profile** — $0 plan with one searchable service and agency portfolio/reviews features.
7. **DesignRush Free profile** — free listing exists; use only the free profile, no sponsorship.
8. **GitHub repository** — owned branded result; maintain a complete README linking to the official website and core service pages.

## Consistency rules

- Always use the exact brand name **The Limo Marketing Agency**.
- Use the same logo where a platform supports it.
- Use the official website URL consistently.
- Do not invent a US or UK office, street address or headquarters.
- If a platform requires a location, use only a truthful business/operating location supplied by the owner.
- Do not claim NLA, PHTM, Google Partner or other memberships/partnerships unless verified and active.
- Do not upload fabricated reviews or create self-reviews.
- Do not create keyword-stuffed business names such as `The Limo Marketing Agency SEO Google Ads USA`.
- After each profile is live, record the public URL before adding it to website `sameAs` structured data.

## Measurement

Track:
- branded Google impressions for `The Limo Marketing Agency` and close variants;
- how many first-page results are controlled/earned brand assets;
- referral sessions and qualified inquiries from each profile;
- newly discovered referring domains;
- whether Google begins associating the brand with limo marketing, limo SEO, limo Google Ads, limo website design and limo booking systems.

Do not treat a profile creation as proof of ranking impact. Measure the effect over time.
