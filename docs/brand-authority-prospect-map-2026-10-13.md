# TLMA Brand Authority Prospect Map

Updated: 2026-10-13

Purpose: build a legitimate, industry-relevant authority footprint for The Limo Marketing Agency across chauffeured transportation, taxi/private-hire, luxury ground transportation and supporting technology ecosystems.

## Operating rules

1. Relevance over volume. One real industry mention is more valuable than dozens of generic directory links.
2. No PBNs, bulk backlink packages, automated guest-post networks, fake reviews or paid links disguised as editorial.
3. Do not claim membership, partnership, integration, sponsorship or client relationship until it is actually approved and documented.
4. Editorial outreach and paid/vendor membership are separate tracks. A membership fee is not treated as a ranking purchase.
5. Exact-match anchor requests are prohibited. Use natural brand/entity anchors.
6. A sent pitch is not a backlink. Record the final live URL, context, destination page, crawlability and referral traffic only after publication.
7. Named client stories remain permission-gated under `docs/client-proof-ledger.md`.

# Outreach order

## Wave 1 — Earned authority

Continue follow-up on the four already-sent editorial pitches. Do not resend immediately. Follow up only after a reasonable editorial response window and with a useful additional angle, not a generic “checking in.”

## Wave 2 — Association qualification

Before paying anything, confirm for NLA, TTA, CLTA, CTANJ, ILLBA, NLTA and GLTA:

- eligibility for a marketing/technology agency;
- whether a public company profile or directory listing is included;
- whether the listing is crawlable and publicly accessible;
- member audience size / relevance;
- event, webinar, newsletter or education opportunities;
- total annual cost;
- cancellation / renewal terms;
- whether members can contribute educational content without purchasing advertising.

## Wave 3 — Ecosystem relationships

Approach software and operations vendors only with a concrete value proposition, such as:

- implementation or onboarding support;
- operator marketing education;
- conversion/booking UX research;
- co-authored operator guides;
- anonymized industry benchmark studies;
- technically accurate integration/booking workflow content.

No “please add us to your partners page” outreach without a real relationship.

# Destination-page strategy

Earned links should point naturally to the most useful asset, not always the homepage.

- Editorial SEO topic → `/seo-for-limo-companies.html` or future audit checklist
- Google Ads topic → `/google-ads-for-limo-companies.html` or the budget guide
- Booking / dispatch topic → `/booking-systems.html` or future UK pricing research
- Company/entity mention → homepage
- Verified client implementation story → `/case-studies-results.html`

# Measurement

For every live authority placement record:

- source/domain
- type: editorial / association / ecosystem / profile
- date earned
- live URL
- target TLMA URL
- anchor/context
- dofollow/nofollow/sponsored if exposed
- crawl/index status
- referral visits
- assisted leads where measurable
- branded-query movement
- relevant keyword movement

Do not claim a ranking increase was caused by one link unless there is a controlled basis for that conclusion.
