# TLMA Association Qualification Outreach Pack

Updated: 2026-10-13

Purpose: qualify legitimate transportation-industry associations before spending money or claiming any membership. These are research-ready templates only. Do not send without explicit outreach approval.

## Core qualification questions

Every association/vendor-membership inquiry should answer the following before TLMA pays or publishes a member claim:

1. Is a specialist digital marketing / booking-technology agency eligible for supplier, vendor, solution-provider or trade membership?
2. Does membership include a publicly accessible company profile or member/vendor directory listing?
3. Is that profile visible without a member login?
4. Can the profile link to the member's website?
5. Are educational contributions, webinars, articles, panels, newsletters or member resources available to suppliers without buying advertising?
6. What operator segments and geographic markets make up the membership?
7. What are the full annual dues and any initiation, directory, event or affinity-program fees?
8. Are renewal terms automatic, and what is the cancellation window?
9. Is sponsorship or advertising required for directory inclusion or educational participation?
10. Who is the best contact for supplier/member education or partnership opportunities?

## Standard positioning

Use consistent entity language:

**The Limo Marketing Agency** is a specialist growth and booking-infrastructure agency serving limousine, chauffeur, black-car, airport-transportation and private-hire operators. Core capabilities include Google Ads, SEO/GEO, conversion-focused websites, booking workflows and digital operations support.

Do not add client counts, revenue claims, association credentials, partner badges or quantified outcomes unless they are verified and approved.

# Outreach cadence

- Do not send all association inquiries in one burst.
- Start with the highest relevance / lowest commitment qualification targets.
- Record date sent, recipient, reply, fees, eligibility, public profile availability and educational opportunities.
- If there is no reply, use one useful follow-up after a reasonable response window rather than repeated generic nudges.
- Never pay before the benefits and renewal terms are documented.

## Recommended first qualification wave after approval

1. TTA
2. CLTA
3. CTANJ
4. ILLBA

NLTA and GLTA can follow after the first responses clarify whether regional association participation is producing meaningful operator access.

## Success criteria

An association is worth prioritizing when at least two of these are true:
- highly relevant operator membership;
- legitimate public entity/profile signal;
- education/editorial opportunities;
- events or direct operator networking;
- credible referral or partnership path;
- reasonable cost relative to the commercial opportunity.

A directory link by itself is not sufficient justification for membership.
