## Suggested qualification workflow for owner-approved outreach

1. **Manual website review**: check the home/airport journey, booking CTA label and what the public form explicitly promises. No unannounced submissions.
2. **Evidence capture**: note 1–2 grounded observations with exact page URL and observation date. Do not diagnose low ROI, weak SEO, poor speed, missing Google Ads conversion tracking or abandoned customer journeys without direct evidence.
3. **Fit**: identify whether booking automation, quote-flow clarity or SEO/Ads measurement *may* be relevant. If the site already offers full transparent instant pricing/confirmation, treat it as a benchmarking opportunity instead of a presumed defect.
4. **Approach**: write an individual 80–120 word non-promotional note with one useful observation and optional link to TLMA's existing 12-point conversion worksheet. Never claim an agency-client relationship, invented results or exclusive industry recognition.
5. **Contact gate**: owner review/approval before sending; use lawful business outreach practices and appropriate opt-out. Do not infer that listing a contact address means outreach is desired.
6. **Outcome logging**: sent / response / interested / call booked / qualified / signed; use only verifiable source records. Avoid duplicate messages to companies already covered elsewhere.

## Reusable hypothesis without pretending a problem exists

A *request* is not the same as a *quote*, and neither is a *confirmed booking*. A helpful operator audit asks what is measured at each stage. It should respect operators who intentionally manually approve high-value rides.
