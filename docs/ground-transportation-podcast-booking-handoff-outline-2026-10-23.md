## Segment outline (proposed, not a booked show)

1. **Start with a question (1–2 min):** A website form says “Submitted.” Is that a booking? No: it is a *request received* only when the system and operator actually receive it.
2. **Five honest stages (4–5 min):**
   - **Request received** — trip requirements captured with successful delivery and a request identifier.
   - **Quote prepared/sent** — price and assumptions approved by operator and shared with customer.
   - **Customer response** — accepted, declined, needs changes or no response, with clear timestamps.
   - **Reservation confirmed** — operator confirms capacity, driver/vehicle/service terms and booking status.
   - **Ride delivered/closed** — reconciled outcome; any paid status is separately validated against the payment provider where integrated.
   **Never label a quote sent or a GA4 `generate_lead` event as a paid ride.**
3. **Operational handoffs to inspect (3–4 min):** form → owner/admin inbox, operator queue → quote, quote sent → customer reply, confirmed booking → dispatch. Check fallback when email is undelivered, phone unreachable, quote changes, a round trip requires special handling, or two submissions duplicate.
4. **Measurement questions (3 min):** time to first human/automation acknowledgment, time to quote, proportion of received requests quoted, proportion of quoted requests confirmed, percentage of events tied to actual ride types/sources. Define denominator and exclude internal tests. Tracking clicks/forms is useful but *not* a revenue result.
5. **Illustrative case (2–3 min):** clearly label as a fictional example with no genuine customer identity or business KPI: a passenger requests JFK→Manhattan, 6 passengers, 2 bags. Operator reviews details and creates a quote. Customer chooses to accept, but until operator confirmation exists, do not call it booked. A hypothetical $228 quote must not be portrayed as a charged payment or proof of booking.
6. **Takeaway (1–2 min):** owners can complete the [free 12-point booking conversion audit](https://osamaghaffar-the-specialist.github.io/the-limo-marketing-agency/resources.html#limo-booking-conversion-audit) to identify the single missing handoff before buying another ad, tool or system.
