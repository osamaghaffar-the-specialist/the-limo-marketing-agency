# TLMA Brand Authority Prospect Map — US + UK

**Date:** 2026-10-12  
**Purpose:** Build a controlled, evidence-backed authority pipeline for The Limo Marketing Agency (TLMA). The objective is not bulk backlink acquisition. The objective is to earn legitimate third-party mentions, editorial citations, industry participation, supplier visibility, interviews, educational contributions and entity profiles that would still be valuable if search engines ignored the link.

## Operating rules

1. Prefer editorial contribution, interviews, case studies, webinars, research citations and real industry participation over generic link requests.
2. Never buy bulk backlinks, PBN links, automated guest posts, exact-match anchors, fake reviews or fake memberships.
3. Never claim a partnership, membership, integration, award or client result before it is real and verifiable.
4. Paid memberships/directories are evaluated on industry access, referral potential and real visibility — not DA/DR.
5. Vendor case studies stay on HOLD until a real implementation/integration exists.
6. Use the correct TLMA destination URL for the topic; do not point every mention to the homepage.
7. Track brand-search growth, referring domains, referral traffic, GSC impressions/clicks and qualified leads separately. Never claim one backlink caused a ranking change.

## Tier definitions

- **Tier A — Earned editorial / expert authority:** highest priority. Pitch useful, non-promotional content, interviews, research, commentary or case studies.
- **Tier B — Industry associations / member ecosystems:** qualify supplier/vendor membership, education, webinars, newsletters, directories and events.
- **Tier C — Vendor / software ecosystems:** pursue only where TLMA has a truthful implementation angle or an independent research contribution.
- **Tier D — Entity / agency profiles:** use selectively to strengthen brand/entity consistency and lead discovery; do not mass-submit.

## Brand authority KPIs

The authority sprint should be judged by a combination of:
1. exact/near-exact `The Limo Marketing Agency` branded queries;
2. number of legitimate independent brand mentions;
3. relevant referring domains;
4. editorial/association appearances;
5. growth of the four GSC topic clusters: Core Limo Marketing, Limo SEO, Limo Google Ads, Limo Website & Booking;
6. organic inquiries / booked calls;
7. page-one commercial keyword count.

No legitimate plan can guarantee immediate #1 rankings. The goal is to systematically increase the signals required to compete for them: relevance, crawl/index adoption, original value, third-party authority, proof, branded demand and conversion performance.
