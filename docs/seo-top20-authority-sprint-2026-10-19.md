## Priority B — One genuinely useful contribution angle (non-promotional)

**Working asset:** `From Google Click to Confirmed Limo Booking: 12-Point Attribution & Dispatch Handoff Audit`.

Original checklist; useful even if TLMA receives no backlink:

| # | Audit check | Evidence to collect | Decision supported |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Match landing page to actual trip/service intent | Query/search term and landing URL | Does the enquiry fit the operator? |
| 2 | Determine which customer action matters | Calls, form completion, instant booking | What is a genuine conversion? |
| 3 | Confirm success-only conversion fires | DebugView plus successful form/booking record | Are fake/direct-thank-you leads excluded? |
| 4 | Detect repeated events or refresh double-counting | Same session/order ID and event timestamps | Is lead volume overstated? |
| 5 | Capture minimum booking details | Pickup/drop-off, date/time, passengers, luggage | Can dispatch/quotes be accurate? |
| 6 | Distinguish enquiry from quote | Lead creation and quote-issued timestamps | Are leads being mistaken for bookings? |
| 7 | Measure first operator response | Submission-to-response duration | Are warm enquiries going cold? |
| 8 | Track quote acceptance | Quote ID and accepted/declined status | What fraction of quotes progress? |
| 9 | Track confirmed bookings distinctly | Reservation ID and confirmation time | What business outcome is paid search delivering? |
| 10 | Exclude cancelled/unpaid reservations from paid-booking totals when appropriate | Cancellation/payment fields | Is return overstated? |
| 11 | Attribute conservatively | Source/medium, click IDs, consent constraints | Where do reporting blind spots exist? |
| 12 | Report by business stage | Cost per lead, cost per quote, cost per confirmed booking | Which bottleneck is worth fixing first? |

This is a workflow audit/checklist, **not** a claim that every system already supports every event or that these recommendations guarantee revenue.

Editorial synopsis (for internal use only):
> Ground-transportation owners often have separate numbers for ad clicks, website enquiries and actual booked trips. A twelve-check operational audit can show where those numbers stop matching: form success events, quote response time, acceptance, dispatch handoff and confirmation. The piece provides a practical worksheet and examples without promising specific growth or fabricating client data.

Potential future location: expand existing `/resources.html` or refine existing `docs/limo-seo-technical-audit-checklist-brief.md` after indexing stabilizes. No new page is released in this sprint.
