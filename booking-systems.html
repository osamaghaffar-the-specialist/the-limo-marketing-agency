Booking System FAQ

Understand the System Before We Build It.

These are the questions that usually matter most to a limo operator deciding whether to move from manual inquiry handling to a connected booking and operations workflow.

What is limo booking and reservation software?
Our documented custom workflow gathers trip details, records customer requests and supports operator-created quotations and notifications. Automated customer pricing, checkout and confirmation are additional integrations that must be scoped and verified separately.
Is this just a booking form?
No. The customer request can connect to an operator-facing quotation and booking record. Other modules, including online card payments, require an agreed integration scope and testing.
Does the customer see the price before paying?
An operator can prepare and send a quote. Automatic customer-facing fares before checkout are possible only when the pricing engine and any payment steps have been configured and tested for that project.
Does the customer have to wait for us to send a quote?
In the documented workflow, an operator can review and send a quote. Removing that manual step for standard trips would require a separately configured and verified automated-pricing workflow.
How does card payment work?
Secure payment processing is an optional project integration, not a proven active checkout on this page. The payment provider, authorization, webhooks, confirmations and failure states must be agreed and tested before launch.
What happens immediately after successful payment?
If a project includes a tested payment processor and confirmation workflow, payment events can update the reservation record and trigger customer/operator notifications. This page does not represent a recorded live charge.
Can the system handle airport, point-to-point, round trip and hourly bookings?
Yes. Each ride type can present the inputs and pricing logic appropriate to that service instead of forcing every customer through the same generic form.
Can we use our own airport rates and hourly prices?
Yes. The system is designed around your commercial rules. Airport flat rates, P2P logic, hourly rates, round-trip logic, additional stops, gratuity and applicable fees can be configured around the way your company prices rides.
Can customers choose a vehicle?
Yes. Customer-facing fleet options can include the relevant vehicle details, capacity and presentation, and the selected vehicle is carried into the booking record.
What can we manage from the Admin Portal?
The documented capability covers booking requests, quotation handling and related data. Fleet information, pricing administration, payment status and draft workflows can be scoped as further modules rather than assumed to be delivered in every installation.
What is a Draft Booking?
A Draft is an incomplete booking or quotation workflow record. Depending on the agreed implementation, it can help operators identify requests that still need review or follow-up.
Can customers receive booking notifications?
Yes. Email and SMS notifications can be connected to important booking events so the customer and the business receive the updates configured for the workflow.
Does automation replace our staff?
No. It removes repetitive work from standard bookings. Your team still manages real operations, exceptions, service quality and customer situations that require human judgment.
Can this work with our existing website?
It can be designed as part of a new website or integrated into an existing digital presence, depending on the current technology and the booking experience you want customers to have.
How much time can this save?
Time savings depend on the actual workflow, volume and integrations delivered. Structured requests and operator quotations can reduce manual re-entry; payment and instant-confirmation time savings should not be claimed without real measured deployment evidence.
