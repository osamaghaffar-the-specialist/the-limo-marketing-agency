Booking System FAQ

Questions Limo Operators Usually Ask before We Build

Can the system handle airport, hourly, round-trip and point-to-point bookings?
Yes. The booking flow can treat each ride type differently so the customer sees the correct inputs, pricing logic and operational details for that trip.
Can I use fixed airport rates and different pricing for other rides?
Yes. Airport flat rates can coexist with point-to-point, hourly, zone, distance or other custom pricing rules.
Do I have to show prices instantly?
No. The system can be quote-first, instant-price, or a mix depending on the trip type and how you prefer to sell rides.
Can customers choose a vehicle based on passenger and luggage capacity?
Yes. Vehicle options can be filtered using the ride requirements so the customer is not shown an unsuitable vehicle.
What does the admin portal control?
The portal can cover bookings, drafts, quotes, customer details, vehicles, pricing rules and the operational information your team needs to manage each request.
Can payments be added?
Yes. A payment-ready checkout can be integrated when the pricing and sales model are appropriate. Quote-first workflows can also stay operator controlled.
