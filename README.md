## Industry focus

The agency is built specifically around limo companies, chauffeur companies, black-car services, airport transportation operators and adjacent private-hire businesses. The website and research are organized around the real commercial journeys these operators care about: visibility, qualified traffic, quote and booking flow, payment, measurement and operational handoff.

## SEO and authority principles

- One primary search intent per important URL.
- No mass city-page cloning or doorway-page strategy.
- No fabricated case studies, reviews, prices, partnerships or performance guarantees.
- Original research, transparent methodology and permission-based proof are preferred over generic content volume.
- Search visibility is measured against qualified inquiries and booking actions, not rankings alone.
